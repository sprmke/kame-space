Finding Talk to Kame

Disclosure and naming

Before, the panel said "AI voice receptionist" and "captions may be stored". After, it is "Kame, your AI receptionist", with Gemini processing, audio not stored, captions saved with the call and deletable, and "AI can make mistakes".

The call itself

Before, the app bar, chat header, stay strip and tab bar stayed live, the timer was an unlabelled number, and the content sat at the top. After, the call takes over, the timer reads time left, turns amber under a minute with "About a minute left.", and the booth is centred. A3

Delete captions

Before, one tap removed the transcript. After, the same tap opens "Delete captions?" with Keep and a red Delete. At every width the dialog stays readable and above the panel.

B4

Message host

Before, the footer button ended the call immediately. After, it opens "End the call?" with Keep talking and End call and message. The control stays beside mute at every width.

A4

Microphone blocked

Before, the header blamed the connection while the body named the microphone. After, the header says "Microphone blocked" and matches the body at every width.

B2

Super admin tabs

Before, five tabs wrapped and the active tab read like a stray button at 375 and 768. After, one row scrolls and keeps the active tab in view. At 1024 and 1440 nothing wraps either way.

B1

Super admin numbers
