Guest journey review: what to fix, what to skip

Search to deposit refund, checked at phone, tablet, desktop and dark mode. 27 findings. This board is the decision sheet: each item shows the screen the guest actually sees, what is wrong, the change I propose, and whether I would do it now.

Read this first. Two of the three top items are not design taste. They are money and trust: the guest is asked to pay without seeing the total, and asked where to send their refund without seeing the amount. The third is a promise the marketing page makes and the product does not keep. Everything else is craft.

Must fix first 3 items

Each of these either costs a booking or hides money from the guest. All three are contained changes, no schema work.

G1Must fix Small, but a decision

The form asks for an account, the home page says "No account needed"

What the guest sees

They tap Reserve on a listing, pick dates, and land on a login wall. The home page step still reads "Pick your dates, add your guests and a valid ID. No account needed."

What I would change

One decision, then one small edit. Either keep the account requirement and rewrite that sentence plus the FAQ, or allow a true guest booking. The code already documents the gate as intentional, so this is copy and product, not a bug hunt.

Do now: tell me which way to go, I will do the copy pass in the same change.
G2Must fix Small

The refund step never shows the refund amount

What the guest sees

Three payout options and a submit button. No deposit amount, no deductions, no total coming back, no payout time. The number was shown days earlier on the trip page.

What I would change

Add one summary line above the options: security deposit, deductions if any, refund total. Add the payout window near the button instead of only on the done screen.

Do now: contained UI change, data is already in the payload.
G3Must fix Small to medium

The payment step hides what the stay costs

What the guest sees

"Downpayment ₱2,000" and "Total due now ₱2,000". The nightly rate, the stay total, the deposit and the balance on arrival are all missing.

What I would change

Always show the full breakdown, not only when a voucher applies: rate times nights, deposit, downpayment, balance due later. The trip page already proves the numbers exist.

Do now: highest trust impact of the whole list.

Cheap confusion fixes 10 items, all small

Each is a few hours at most and removes a moment where the guest has to guess. I would batch these right after the top three.

G4Confusing Small

Same form, two shells, one without a progress indicator

What the guest sees

Booking from the listing opens a sheet titled "Guest Form" with no step count. The full-page version of the same form shows five steps.

Change

Put the compact "Guest, 1 of 5" line in the sheet header.

Do now: one header component, reused.
G6Confusing Small

Disabled buttons look enabled

What the guest sees

This "Select dates" is disabled. It still looks like the live teal button. The same happens with "Submit guest form" while the policy box is unticked: it stays enabled and the click does nothing visible.

Change

Give disabled its own grey surface, and wire submit to the agreement.

Do now: token plus one guard.
G5Confusing Medium

Guests cannot tell which nights are free

What the guest sees

Four visual states (past, booked, available, today) are all pale numbers, with no key anywhere. Screen readers get the right words, sighted guests get nothing.

Change

Add a compact legend and give booked nights a real treatment, not just lower opacity.

Next pass: it needs a small design decision, not just code.
G7Dead end Medium

Expired links are dead ends in developer language

What the guest sees

"Review unavailable. Access token required." with no button at all. The refund form offers only "Back to home". The trip page says "Booking not found" for a booking that does exist, because the guest is signed out.

Change

One shared expired-link screen: plain words, "Send me a new link", "Message the host".

Next pass: needs a reset-link endpoint, not only UI.
G8Confusing Small

"SD Refund Form" is internal shorthand

What the guest sees

A title, a done screen and an email that all say "SD". Guests know it as the security deposit.

Change

"Security deposit refund" on guest surfaces, "SD" stays for host screens.

Do now: string change only.
G9Wrong copy Small

The voucher thanks the guest for a review they declined

What the guest sees

They tapped "Not now", then read "Thanks for your review!".

Change

Branch the line on whether a review was submitted.

Do now: two-line conditional.
G10Inconsistent Small

"Step 3 of 5" fills about 2.4 of 5 bars

What the guest sees

The label and the bar disagree, so they cannot tell if they are behind.

Change

Drive the fill from step over total, or drop the number.

Do now: one expression.
G11Inconsistent Small

"What happens next" has no done state

What the guest sees

Three rows that all look the same. Only a dotted ring hints at in-progress.

Change

Filled check for done, ring for in-progress, muted outline for upcoming, plus a status word.

Do now: same component, three variants.
G12Trust Medium

The booking card never previews the total

What the guest sees

Rate per night, dates, guests, Reserve. No stay total, no refundable deposit, no fee line.

Change

After dates are chosen, show the stay total and the deposit before the form.

Next pass: overlaps G3, better done in the same sweep.
G13Accessibility Small

Rating stars are about 20px

What the guest sees

Small targets, and in the six category rows they sit at the far edge of a phone screen.

Change

44px hit area per star, or a segmented 1 to 5 control on phone.

Do now: house rule already says 44px.
G14Visual bug Small

The "Parkings" chip is cut in half

What the guest sees

The chip row runs under the round filter button, mid-word, with no fade or scroll hint.

Change

Trailing padding plus the documented fade, or move the button to its own row.

Do now: spacing fix.
G15Visual bug Small

The host name truncates to "Hosted by Kame ..."

What the guest sees

An ellipsis where the host name should be. It reads like a broken layout.

Change

Allow two lines, or put "Hosted by" on its own line.

Do now: one class.
G16Visual bug Small

The draft banner covers the property header on phone

What the guest sees

A floating card hiding the header, with "Saved 11/23/2026, 5:42:05 AM" in US order with seconds.

Change

Make it a normal row below the header and format the time for Manila.

Do now: layout plus a date helper.
