Guest flow fixes: before and after

Same script, same seed data, same viewports, run against a clean copy of the code before the change and against the fixed build after. Every pair below is the guest's actual screen, captured by guestFlowVisualCapture.spec.ts.

Before After 15 fixes, 17 before/after pairs, plus a 375 / 768 / 1440 sweep
Reproduce either side: AUDIT_PHASE=before bun run audit:guest-flow-shots against a HEAD copy, then AUDIT_PHASE=after bun run audit:guest-flow-shots on the branch.

G1Home page: the booking promise now matches the product

The step used to say "No account needed" while the form required an account. It now says what actually happens, and the sign-in screen explains why it appeared.

G1Sign-in wall: one line of context

The guest arrives from a listing. Instead of a bare "Log in or sign up", the dialog now says what the account is for.

G3Payment step: the guest finally sees the stay total

Before: downpayment and total due, nothing else. After: estimated stay, refundable security deposit, downpayment, total due now, plus a line saying the host confirms the final figures.

G6Date picker: a disabled button looks disabled

"Select dates" was disabled but still rendered as a live teal gradient. Disabled now has its own neutral surface, so the state reads without hovering or clicking.

G6Submit stays disabled until the policy is accepted

Before, Submit looked live while the agreement was unticked and the click did nothing. Now the button is visibly disabled until the box is ticked, and the checkbox has a 44px row.

G4Booking sheet: the guest knows where they are

The sheet used to say only "Guest Form". It now shows the step and position ("Guest · 1 of 5"), matching the full-page form.

G8No more "SD" shorthand on guest screens

"SD Refund Form" became "Security deposit refund", in the page title, the embed preview and the done screen.

G2Refund step: the guest sees the amount

Before, the guest chose where to send the money with no idea how much. After, a refund summary sits above the options: deposit, deductions, total, and when it is sent.

G2Same refund summary on phone

The amount block is the first thing under the stepper, above the payout options, so it is in view without scrolling.

G9Voucher no longer thanks a guest who skipped the review

"Not now" used to land on "Thanks for your review!". The line is now conditional on a review actually existing; the offer copy is unchanged.

G10 · G11Trip page: progress matches the label, checklist states are readable

"Step 3 of 5" used to fill about 2.4 of five segments. The current step now fills its segment at lower emphasis, so three of five read as done plus current. Each checklist row also states "Done" or "To do" instead of relying on the circle alone.

The trip page needs a real booking, so both sides use the local seed (booking ending 0058). The before frame is the audit-pass capture of the same page and booking.

G13Category stars: bigger and aligned with their labels

The six category rows used 18px stars pushed left of the label by a negative margin. They are now 22px and sit on the same left edge as "Cleanliness", "Accuracy" and the rest. (The tap target was already 44px; the audit wording overstated this one.)

G14Search chips: the last category is no longer sliced

The chip row ran under the filter button and cut "Parkings" mid-word. The scroller now reserves trailing room so the last chip clears the button.

G15Listing: the host name wraps instead of vanishing

"Hosted by Kame ..." was truncated mid-name on a phone. The line now wraps to a second line.

G16Draft restore: out of the header, and a readable time

The restore prompt used to cover the property header for 15 seconds and read "11/24/2026, 12:40:18 PM". It now sits at the bottom, leaving the header visible, and reads as a short Manila time such as "Nov 24, 12:39 PM".

G5Calendar: a key for what the numbers mean

Available, booked and past days were all pale numbers with no explanation. The calendar now shows a two-item key (Available, Booked) whenever the visible month actually has a booked night, and booked days keep their strikethrough. Renders at phone, tablet and desktop.

G7Expired link: plain words and a way forward

Before: "Form not available / Access token required / Back to home". After: "This link has expired", one sentence telling the guest what to do, and two actions (Message the host, Back to home). Same screen on the guest review page, which previously had no action at all.
